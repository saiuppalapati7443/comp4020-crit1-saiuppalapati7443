Sightings

This page works like a guestbook, in spirit if not in function. There is no form here --- this site is pure HTML and CSS, with no JavaScript behind it, so nothing can actually be submitted from this page. What follows are sightings as I imagine them being reported, written in the voice of the people who might report them. If you have a real sighting of your own, the about page explains how to reach me.

  • A red umbrella, abandoned, Platform 2. Spotted leaning against a bench at the far end of the platform, fully closed, looking patient rather than lost. Nobody came back for it in the twenty minutes I waited for my train.
  • A striped umbrella, open, stuck in a hedge. Blew clean out of someone's hand on a windy corner and ended up three gardens down, caught in a hedge like it had simply decided to retire there. I did not retrieve it. Some umbrellas choose their own ending.
  • A black umbrella, inside out, in a bin. The most common kind of sighting there is. Nobody mourns these the way they mourn the distinctive ones, which I think is its own kind of unfairness.
  • A clear dome umbrella, left on a cafe chair. Sat there through an entire lunch service, untouched, while the chair around it got used by three different people. The staff eventually moved it to a shelf by the door, where it presumably still is.
  • A golf umbrella, enormous, propped by a lecture hall door. Too big to be anyone's accident --- someone made a deliberate choice to leave this behind rather than carry it inside. I respect the decision even if I do not understand it.
